Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pest control treatments and safety procedures.

Are your pest control chemicals safe for pets and children?
Yes, absolutely. We use 100% government-approved odorless herbal gels and low-toxicity eco-friendly formulations. Children, pets, and elderly family members can remain safely in the house during and after our cockroach and ant gel treatments.
Do I need to empty my kitchen cabinets for cockroach gel treatment?
No! That is the beauty of our advanced German gel baiting technique. Our technicians apply minute gel dots in hidden hinges and corners without requiring you to move a single utensil or container.
How does the 5-Year Termite Warranty work?
Our anti-termite treatment includes precision drilling along walls and furniture joints, filling with high-grade termiticide, and sealing with white cement. We provide an official stamped warranty certificate. If any termite activity resurfaces within 5 years, we re-treat the area free of charge.
What are the advantages of taking an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)?
An AMC guarantees scheduled periodic checkups (every 2 to 3 months) to destroy pest breeding cycles before they start. Plus, AMC subscribers get unlimited free emergency callbacks, free sanitization sessions, and discounted rates.
How quickly can a technician visit my location?
We provide same-day service! For emergency calls, our technicians arrive within 2 hours across Mumbai, Thane, and Navi Mumbai.
